Industry Standard

e-NAM (National Agriculture Market)

"e-NAM primarily provides an electronic agricultural market and price discovery across physical integrated mandis."
  • ❌ Reactive trading: farmer must harvest and transport produce to APMC mandi first.
  • ❌ Single-seller to single-buyer auction; does not bundle 5 smallholders to fulfill 1 corporate demand.
  • ❌ No local demand predictive forecasting before crops are harvested.
  • ❌ Inbound/outbound logistics are listed, but no multi-stop collection route optimization is solved.
Private Marketplace

AgriBazaar

"AgriBazaar provides digital trade, price negotiation, and warehouse logistics."
  • ❌ Focuses on fulfillment logistics after bilateral agreements are already made.
  • ❌ One-on-one negotiation without automated multi-farm price band benchmarking.
  • ❌ Does not identify local aggregate supply gaps before transaction execution.
  • ❌ Lacks unified Traveling Salesperson route scheduling across farm gates.
Our Solution

AgriConnect AI Platform

"Our prototype focuses on the intelligence layer before fulfillment: predictive supply-demand coordination at the local level."
  • ✅ Predictive Demand: Forecasts upcoming local demand 7–14 days ahead using ML regression with day-of-week seasonality.
  • ✅ Multi-Farmer Supply Aggregation: Automatically bundles multiple smallholders to satisfy large bulk requirements.
  • ✅ Instant Shortage Detection: Pinpoints regional deficits immediately to alert buyers and nearby clusters.
  • ✅ Fair Negotiation Engine: Algorithmic counter-offer recommender that protects farmer floor margins.
  • ✅ Dynamic Route TSP Optimization: Computes optimal multi-stop collection loops, cutting road mileage by up to 40% and saving CO2 emissions.
